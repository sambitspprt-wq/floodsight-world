From signal to action

A clearer view of urban water risk.

FloodSight follows a workflow operators understand: ingest, enrich, predict, alert.

01

Ingest

Live weather and rainfall observations streamed from Open-Meteo for the selected location — updated every few minutes.

02

Enrich

Terrain, land-use and historical flood signals encoded as a per-city flood-proneness profile.

03

Predict

Configurable, transparent thresholds classify each zone's risk from 0–100% — every factor is traceable.

04

Alert

Clear, traceable warnings with a lifecycle status: detected → triaged → active.

FloodSight · World — a software-only urban flood-forecasting prototype. Risk scores are heuristic estimates built from live weather plus static city profiles, and are not an official flood warning. Always follow your national meteorological service and local disaster-authority advisories.
Data: Open-Meteo  ·  Thresholds: standard heavy-rainfall categories
